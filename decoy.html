Reading Response: Sediment and Time

Due Friday · 20 points · Not turned in

Directions

Read the passage below, then answer the four questions in complete sentences. Cite at least one detail from the text in each response.

Passage

A riverbed keeps a slow and careless record. Each spring the water rises, carries loose material downstream, and drops it again where the current weakens. The heavier grains settle first, near the bend; the finer ones travel further before they come to rest. Over many seasons these deposits stack into bands, and the bands differ from one another in colour and coarseness depending on what the river was carrying that year.

A geologist reading such a bank is not looking at a story that anyone intended to tell. Nothing about the arrangement was deliberate. Still, the order is reliable: lower bands were laid down before the ones above them, and a thick band suggests a season of heavy flow. From this a rough calendar can be assembled, though it is a calendar with gaps, because a dry year leaves very little behind and a flood can strip away what earlier years deposited.

This is the ordinary difficulty of reading any record that was not written for a reader. The evidence is genuine, but it is uneven, and the quietest periods tend to leave the faintest trace.

Questions

  1. In your own words, explain why heavier grains settle earlier than finer ones.
  2. The passage says the riverbed keeps a "careless" record. What does the author mean by this?
  3. Why might a dry year be difficult to identify in the bank?
  4. The final paragraph draws a wider conclusion. Restate it and give one example of another record that might have the same problem.
