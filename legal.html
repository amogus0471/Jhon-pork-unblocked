Notices & takedown

Plain-language summary of what this site is, what it hosts, and how to get something removed.

What this site is

A personal, non-commercial index of browser games. It is a hobby project. There is no advertising, no payment, no subscription, and nothing is sold here.

No affiliation

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or connected to any game developer, publisher, studio, school, or software vendor. All product names, game titles, logos and trademarks belong to their respective owners and are used only to identify the games in question. Use of a name does not imply any relationship.

Third-party content

The games listed here were not created by the operator of this site. They come from third-party collections. Two things follow from that:

  • Modules marked Hosted are not stored here at all. They load from someone else's server, and this site simply links to them.
  • Modules marked Verified are stored here so they load reliably. No claim of ownership or authorship is made over any of them.

Thumbnail images are frames captured from the modules as they run, used to identify each entry in the catalog. No promotional or marketing artwork has been copied from any publisher.

Takedown requests

If you own the rights to something here and want it removed, it will be removed. You do not need to send a formal legal notice first, and there is no intent to dispute a good-faith request.

The fastest route is to open an issue on the repository:

  • github.com/amogus0471/Jhon-pork-unblocked/issues

You can also file a notice directly with the host, GitHub, under their published DMCA process. To help a request be actioned quickly, please include:

  • The module name or URL on this site.
  • Identification of the work you hold rights in.
  • Your contact details.
  • A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised, and that the information in your notice is accurate.

Requests are acted on promptly. A removed module is deleted from the catalog, not merely hidden.

Privacy

No accounts, no tracking pixels, no analytics service, and no advertising network.

  • Your preferences (tab title, escape key, theme choices) are stored only in your own browser using localStorage. They are never transmitted.
  • If the discussion board is enabled, anything you post there is public. A one-way hash of your IP address is used briefly for rate limiting only; the address itself is not stored.
  • The "students in session" figure counts short-lived anonymous session tokens. It holds no personal information.

No warranty

This site is provided as-is, with no warranty of any kind. Modules may break at any time, particularly those loaded from upstream hosts that can disappear without notice. Nothing here is guaranteed to be available, accurate, or fit for any purpose.

Acceptable use

If you are on a network that has rules about what you may access — a school, workplace, or public network — those rules are between you and whoever runs that network. Using this site does not override them, and nothing here should be read as encouragement to breach a policy you have agreed to.

This page is a good-faith plain-English notice written by the site operator. It is not legal advice.
